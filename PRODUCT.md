# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Two kinds of visitor: friends who want a quiet look at who Arun is, and developers or recruiters who want to know what he works on and with. Both should get a feel for him quickly, and the résumé is one click away for anyone who wants more.

## Product Purpose
A small personal portfolio for Arun S R, a developer in Chennai, India. It introduces him in one short passage, points to his résumé and GitHub. Success is a visitor leaving calm, with the résumé or GitHub opened if they wanted more.

## Positioning
A developer portfolio with a personal point of view: a watercolour scene and a pile of dry leaves that can be cracked. Work and skills are stated plainly; it is not a case study index.

## Operating Context
Static Next.js site (App Router, React, TypeScript). Pages: home and a 404. The writing section was removed on request. The résumé is a .pdf in /public, opened in the browser's PDF viewer (a separate résumé page was tried and removed on request). Contact is a mailto link and a Cmd/Ctrl+K command palette.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Primary action: open the résumé. GitHub is secondary (most work is in private repos); Email and LinkedIn are quieter still.
- Keep it minimal and few words. Essentials only.
- SparrowDesk may appear only where it is factual (the experience section). It was removed from the hero and metadata on request; do not put it back there.
- No privacy, terms or footer bar (removed on request).
- The fox painting (public/backgrounds/hero.jpg) and the leaf cracking interaction are signature pieces.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Arun S R. Voice: plain, first person, specific, quiet. The greeting is "Hi, I'm Arun."

## Evidence on Hand
Résumé (public/Arun-S-R-Resume.pdf), GitHub (github.com/arun-121), LinkedIn. No testimonials, metrics, clients or awards exist, so none may be invented.

## Product Principles
- Say less. One sentence beats a section.
- Calm over clever: motion and interaction should feel physical and never get in the way.
- Only true things: no invented claims, roles or numbers.
- The résumé and GitHub are always one click away.
